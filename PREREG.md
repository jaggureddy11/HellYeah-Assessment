## Entry 1 — 2026-11-11 04:41 IST (0:39 elapsed, 3h 21m remaining)

### (a) Objective we will optimize, and why

**Objective:** Maximize total **30-day cumulative revenue** over days 61–90 under the fixed \$2,000/day non-carrying-over budget (equivalently, maximize expected delivered daily revenue given each creative's marginal return and delivery cap).

**Why this objective:**
1. [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) specifies a fixed 30-day horizon at \$2,000/day (\$60,000 total, non-renewing), and [FAQ.md](FAQ.md) confirms evaluation scores **total revenue over days 61–90**.
2. Maximizing raw ROAS (`revenue / spend`) without accounting for budget absorption/delivery ignores unspent budget: in `data/daily_final.csv` (`n = 1,784` rows), overall spend delivery is `92.88%` (`$111,447.33 / $119,992.12`), and at `budget >= $200` (`n = 85` rows) `62.4%` of allocations fail to spend in full (`sum(spend)/sum(budget) = 0.7217`). Unspent budget yields \$0 and does not carry over, so the policy must allocate by **marginal expected revenue per allocated dollar**, not average ROAS alone.

### (b) Empirical phenomena found in `data/`

     - `age 0` (day 60, `n = 40`): `rev_fin/rep = 2.9038` (`$3,475 / $1,197`), `pur_fin/rep = 3.5000` (`49 / 14`).
     - `age 1` (day 59, `n = 40`): `rev_fin/rep = 1.8378`, `pur_fin/rep = 1.9545`.
     - `age 2` (day 58, `n = 40`): `rev_fin/rep = 1.5131`, `pur_fin/rep = 1.4667`.
     - `age 3` (day 57, `n = 40`): `rev_fin/rep = 1.3091`, `pur_fin/rep = 1.3077`.

2. **Delivery shortfalls at higher budgets and zero delivery under \$1**
   - Across `n = 1,784` rows in `daily_final.csv`, `spend < budget` on `91` rows (`5.1%`); `spend > budget` on `0` rows.
   - On rows with `budget < $1` (`n = 51`, all `budget == 0`), `spend = $0.00`, `impressions = 0`, `clicks = 0`, `purchases = 0`, `revenue = $0.00`.

Five creatives account for most shortfalls (`CR008`: 40, `CR031`: 11, `CR024`: 10, `CR028`: 7, `CR037`: 6).

3. **Large creative ROAS dispersion alongside high daily noise**

     - First-half vs Second-half ROAS (`n = 40`): `Pearson r = 0.764 [0.619, 0.864]`, `Spearman r = 0.713 [0.475, 0.843]`, with `Mean(H2 - H1) = -0.302 [-0.490, -0.103]`.

### (d) Falsification criteria — what result would make us drop or change each item in (c)

1. **Lag-aware estimation:** Drop or replace with simple recent-day exclusion (`t - 0`, `t - 1`) if ablation shows lag correction reduces 30-day revenue (or fails to improve over no correction) due to variance inflation on zero-purchase days (`34.7%` of rows).
2. **`format` cold-start prior:** Drop `format` grouping (fall back to global shrinkage prior) if ablation or a shifted world with zero `format` effect causes our policy to underperform global-prior cold start across seeds.
3. **Recency / fatigue weighting:** Lengthen the effective lookback window or flatten decay if recency weighting increases estimation variance enough to lose to an equal-weight window under stationary or noisy worlds.
4. **Diminishing-returns & delivery-cap allocator:** Simplify to hard per-creative share caps (`max_share`) + observed delivery cap if concave marginal optimization adds complexity without improving `(yours - B1) / (clairvoyant - B1)` across seeds.
5. **Cold-start exploration bonus:** Tighten early loser pruning or reduce initial exploration share if cold-start spend on the 10 unserved creatives causes net 30-day revenue loss relative to B2 across worlds.
6. **Simulated environment:** Recalibrate `sim/world_config.json` immediately if `(B2 - B1) / (clairvoyant - B1)` shows negligible headroom (`< 0.10`) or unrealistic signal-to-noise compared to `daily_final.csv` (`creative ROAS sd = 0.797`, `within-creative variance share = 88.0%`).
